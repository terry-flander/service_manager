What owning it means

Most job software is rented space on someone else's platform. KeepCroft is set up for one business at a time, and that business is yours.

Yours alone

  • Your own server, run for your business only
  • Your data, stored with you and nobody else
  • Your own customer portal
  • Bookings and listings on your own website
  • Your own backups

Nobody else's

  • No database shared with unrelated businesses
  • Not one of thousands on a shared platform
  • You decide who can see your data
  • Keeps running on its own if we part ways

Kept up to date

  • Software upgrades included
  • New releases installed for you
  • Set up to fit how your shop already works

The app

Everything a small service business does in a day, in one place, on a phone at the job or a screen at the counter.

Calendar and map

See the week's bookings beside a map of where they are, filtered by day or by who's doing the work.

  • Drag a booking to move it
  • Syncs with Google Calendar
  • Plan runs by suburb and region

Every job, one list

Bookings, workshop jobs, hire and counter sales together, with saved views for the way you like to work.

  • Your own statuses and colours
  • Choose the columns you see
  • Search and filter in a tap

The job card

Parts, labour, status, payment and the whole email conversation with the customer, on one screen.

  • Invoices straight to Xero
  • Email and SMS from the job
  • Assign who's doing the work

Customer portal

Each customer gets a private link to their job: where it's up to, what was done, and the invoice when it's ready.

  • Fewer "is it ready?" calls
  • Online booking from your website
  • Your branding, your address

How it works

  1. We set up your server

    A private server for your business, with your own web address and backups switched on.

  2. We bring your records across

    Customers and past jobs imported, and statuses, job types and staff set up the way you already work.

  3. You run your shop

    New releases are installed for you. Your data stays where it is, on your server.
