Who We Serve

Our Programs

Whether you're a school running Bike Safety education, a business planning a team activity, or a group organising an adventure — we have a program for you.

🏫
Schools

We provide a complete Bike Safety education solution for Melbourne schools. Our fleet arrives with everything your students need for a full 1–2 week program.

  • Fleet of Giant Mountain Bikes sized for your students
  • Helmets and safety equipment supplied
  • Trailer housed at school for the program duration
  • Bikes available at the school's discretion
  • Delivery and pickup covered in the hire cost
  • All bikes safety checked before each deployment
  • Worn parts replaced by our trained mechanics

Simple Process

How It Works

From enquiry to delivery, we handle every detail. You focus on the riding — we take care of everything else.

1
Make an Enquiry Tell us your dates, group size and what you have in mind using the form below.
2
We Confirm Your Booking We'll get back to you quickly to confirm availability and talk through the details.
3
Pre-Delivery Safety Check Every bike in your fleet is inspected by our trained mechanics before loading — worn parts are replaced.
4
We Deliver to You The trailer arrives at your school or site, loaded with bikes and safety equipment, ready to go.
5
We Pick It All Up When your program is complete, we return and collect everything. No fuss.
The Fleet

Giant Mountain Bikes

Our fleet of 24 Giant Mountain Bikes covers a range of sizes to suit different riders — from primary school students through to adults. Every bike is maintained to the same high standard as our workshop customers.

Bikes are loaded and transported in our dedicated enclosed trailer, which is left on-site for the duration of your program so the fleet is always accessible.

Our Commitment

Safety First, Every Time

Every deployment begins with a thorough safety check by our trained mechanics. No bike leaves our trailer unless it meets our standards. Worn or damaged parts are replaced before the next hire — not after.

Pre-Hire Safety Inspection Every bike checked by a qualified mechanic before each deployment
🔩
